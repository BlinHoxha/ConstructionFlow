1. Executive summary

ConstructionFlow AI is a Kosovo-first B2B SaaS platform for small and mid-sized contractors, developers and
construction project managers. It combines project tracking, procurement, document intelligence and site
reporting. Its differentiator is Albanian-first workflows with auditable AI assistance, rather than autonomous
engineering decisions.

· Initial wedge: quotations, invoices, purchase orders and daily site reports.

· Primary buyer: owner/director of a contractor managing 2-10 concurrent projects.

· Economic hypothesis: save 4-10 administrative hours per project per week; validate through timed pilots,
not marketing claims.

· Go/no-go: at least 3 paid pilot commitments and repeatable weekly usage before expanding scope.

Research plan

· Interview 12-20 companies in Prishtina, Prizren, Peja and other markets; stratify by company size and
construction specialty.

· Ask for anonymized sample supplier offers, invoices, BOQs, daily logs and project spreadsheets; measure
baseline process times.

· Map competing alternatives: Excel, WhatsApp, email, accounting software and established construction
SaaS; validate local language and price gaps.

· Assess connectivity at sites, mobile usage, tax-document workflows, approval authority and data
residency preferences.

3. Product and competitive positioning

Position as the operational layer between the construction site, procurement and owner reporting. Do not
attempt to replace CAD, BIM authoring, accounting, structural design or licensed engineering review in the
MVP.

5. Go-to-market strategy

· Phase 0: recruit 5 design partners, preferably 2 contractors, 1 developer, 1 project manager and 1
subcontractor.
· Run 30-day pilots using real but permissioned project documents and explicit data-processing terms.
· Show before/after demonstrations: three supplier quotations processed in minutes, daily report drafted
from a voice note.
· Use referrals from accountants, project managers, engineers and construction suppliers.
· Offer onboarding in Albanian and guided imports from Excel; focus on one city before nationwide
outreach.
· Expand regionally only after localization, regulations and document formats are verified.
